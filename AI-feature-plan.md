# AI Feature Plan

## AI Property Assistant

Support natural language search such as "Find me a 3-bedroom under 8M in Kilimani near schools" by translating user intent into structured filters, ranking signals, and conversational follow-ups.

## AI Listing Generator

Generate listing descriptions, SEO metadata, WhatsApp ads, email campaigns, social captions, and neighborhood-specific selling points. Human approval is required before publishing.

## AI Fraud Detection

Score duplicate listings, suspicious pricing, fake agents, image reuse, document mismatch, and scam patterns. Feed high-risk listings into the moderation queue.

## AI Price Estimator

Estimate rent, sale value, appreciation potential, and rental yield using historical prices, nearby comps, amenities, verification confidence, and demand signals.

## Guardrails

Every AI output should include confidence, source signals, audit records, and human override paths. Legal, valuation, and ownership claims must be presented as decision support, not final certification.
