# Monetization Strategy

## Primary Revenue Streams

1. Premium and boosted listings paid through M-Pesa, Stripe, or Flutterwave.
2. Agent and agency memberships with verified profiles, CRM tools, analytics, and portfolio pages.
3. Lead purchasing and lead subscriptions with anti-spam quality controls.
4. Developer advertising packages for new projects and launches.
5. Investor dashboards for market reports, pricing trends, rental yields, and hotspot analysis.

## Kenya-First Payment Design

M-Pesa should be the default checkout rail. Stripe and Flutterwave should be provider adapters, not payment logic embedded in UI components.

## Trust As The Moat

Verified ownership, KYC, document verification, moderation, and fraud scoring should become monetizable trust signals while protecting buyers and renters.
